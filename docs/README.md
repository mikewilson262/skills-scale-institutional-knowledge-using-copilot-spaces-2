# OctoAcme Project Management Documentation

## Welcome
This folder contains the core guidance for how OctoAcme manages projects and delivers work across the full project lifecycle. The process model is intentionally lightweight, iterative, and role-aware so teams can move quickly while maintaining accountability, visibility, and quality.

## Our Approach
OctoAcme follows a customer-first, iterative project management model grounded in clear ownership, measurable outcomes, and continuous learning. The team starts by validating the business need and defining success metrics, then moves into planning, execution, and release with regular checkpoints for risk management and stakeholder communication. This approach keeps work aligned to customer value, reduces ambiguity, and helps teams improve through retrospectives and evidence-based decision-making.

## Project Management Process Summary
OctoAcme’s project lifecycle is designed to move from idea to delivery in a structured but practical way:

1. Initiation: confirm the business problem, align stakeholders, and decide whether to proceed into planning.
2. Planning: define scope, prioritize the backlog, estimate work, identify dependencies, and agree on milestones and delivery expectations.
3. Execution & Tracking: run the team through standups, coordinate progress, manage blockers, and track delivery against milestones.
4. Risk Management & Communication: identify and monitor risks, assess impacts, communicate status clearly, and escalate when needed.
5. Release & Deployment: validate quality, prepare the release, communicate changes, and ensure a safe deployment with rollback plans when necessary.
6. Retrospective & Continuous Improvement: capture lessons learned, track action items, and improve the process iteratively.

Across all stages, OctoAcme keeps role clarity, stakeholder communication, and quality assurance at the center of delivery.
