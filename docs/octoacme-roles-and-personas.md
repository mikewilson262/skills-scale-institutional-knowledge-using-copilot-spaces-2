# OctoAcme Personas

This document defines typical roles and responsibilities used in OctoAcme project docs and exercises.

---

## Developers

### Role Summary
Developers design, build, test, and deliver software components. They collaborate with product and project leads to implement features that meet acceptance criteria and quality standards.

### Responsibilities
- Implement features and fixes to meet acceptance criteria
- Write and maintain tests and documentation
- Participate in design and code reviews
- Assist in estimating and planning work
- Help identify technical risks and propose mitigations

### Goals
- Deliver reliable, maintainable code
- Reduce cycle time from idea to production
- Maintain high test coverage and observability

### Typical Communication
- Daily standups and sprint planning
- PR descriptions and code review comments
- Technical design docs when needed

---

## Product Managers

### Role Summary
Product Managers define what should be built to deliver customer and business value. They own the product vision, prioritize the backlog, and measure outcomes.

### Responsibilities
- Define problem statements and success metrics
- Prioritize the roadmap and backlog
- Collaborate with stakeholders and engineering on trade-offs
- Validate solutions through user research and metrics

### Goals
- Maximize customer value and impact
- Make clear, data-driven prioritization decisions
- Ensure product-market fit and usability

### Typical Communication
- Weekly alignment with PM and engineering leads
- Roadmap updates and stakeholder briefings
- Acceptance criteria and feature specs

---

## Project Managers

### Role Summary
Project Managers coordinate delivery activities, manage schedules, risks, and communications. They enable the team to deliver on commitments efficiently.

### Responsibilities
- Create and maintain project plans and timelines
- Manage risks, dependencies, and resource constraints
- Facilitate meetings (kickoff, planning, retrospectives)
- Ensure consistent project documentation and status reporting
- Coordinate cross-team and stakeholder communication

### Goals
- Deliver projects on time and within scope
- Minimize unplanned work and escalations
- Maintain transparency and alignment across stakeholders

### Typical Communication
- Weekly status updates and stakeholder reports
- Risk registers and decision logs
- Coordination via project boards and meeting facilitation

---

## QA/Testing Lead

### Role Summary
QA/Testing Leads define and execute quality strategies to ensure products meet acceptance criteria and maintain high quality standards. They collaborate with developers and product teams to validate features and identify defects early.

### Responsibilities
- Develop test plans and QA strategies aligned with project scope
- Define acceptance criteria with Product Managers and Developers
- Execute manual and automated testing throughout the delivery cycle
- Manage defect identification, prioritization, and closure
- Ensure acceptance criteria are met before marking work complete
- Participate in retrospectives to improve testing processes

### Goals
- Ensure products are production-ready before release
- Identify and prevent critical issues
- Reduce post-release defects and customer impact

### Typical Communication
- Sprint planning and retrospectives
- Test case documentation and defect reports
- Sign-off on acceptance criteria

### Interaction with Existing Roles
- **With Developers**: Collaborate on test strategy and acceptance criteria definitions; review test coverage
- **With Project Managers**: Report testing status and quality metrics; escalate quality blockers
- **With Product Managers**: Validate features against acceptance criteria; provide quality feedback on prioritization

---

## Scrum Master / Agile Coach

### Role Summary
Scrum Masters facilitate agile ceremonies and coach teams on continuous improvement. They remove blockers and help the team maintain sustainable velocity.

### Responsibilities
- Facilitate sprint planning, standups, reviews, and retrospectives
- Identify and help resolve team blockers
- Coach team members on agile practices and principles
- Maintain sprint board and communicate progress
- Escalate cross-team dependencies to Project Manager

### Goals
- Keep team focused and productive
- Foster psychological safety and continuous improvement
- Maintain team velocity and predictability

### Typical Communication
- Daily standups and sprint ceremonies
- Retrospective facilitation and action tracking
- 1-on-1 coaching conversations

### Interaction with Existing Roles
- **With Project Managers**: Escalate blockers and dependencies; coordinate cross-team scheduling
- **With Product Managers**: Ensure backlog clarity and sprint readiness
- **With Developers**: Coach on agile practices; remove technical and process blockers

---

## Stakeholders / Sponsors

### Role Summary
Stakeholders and Sponsors provide business context, resources, and approval authority for projects. They are invested in outcomes and help remove organizational barriers.

### Responsibilities
- Approve project charter and key decisions
- Provide business context and success metrics
- Remove organizational blockers
- Participate in milestone reviews and approvals
- Support communication and advocacy for the project

### Goals
- Ensure project delivers business value
- Support team success and resource availability
- Maintain executive visibility and alignment

### Typical Communication
- Project initiation and kickoff
- Monthly stakeholder updates and milestone reviews
- Approval gates and decision forums

### Interaction with Existing Roles
- **With Project Managers**: Approve milestones and manage executive expectations
- **With Product Managers**: Align on business outcomes and priority trade-offs
- **With All Teams**: Provide resources, remove organizational barriers, and escalate needs

---

## Technical Lead / Architect

### Role Summary
Technical Leads define technical strategy and make architecture decisions that align with project goals. They mentor developers and ensure code quality and maintainability.

### Responsibilities
- Define technical architecture and approach
- Review design decisions and technical trade-offs
- Mentor developers on technical best practices
- Identify technical risks and mitigation strategies
- Participate in code reviews and architecture reviews

### Goals
- Deliver maintainable, scalable solutions
- Reduce technical debt and risk
- Build team technical capability

### Typical Communication
- Technical design reviews
- Code reviews and architecture discussions
- Planning and risk mitigation conversations

### Interaction with Existing Roles
- **With Developers**: Mentor on technical practices; review designs and code quality
- **With Project Managers**: Identify technical risks and mitigation approaches
- **With Product Managers**: Advise on technical feasibility and trade-offs

---

## Communications / Release Manager

### Role Summary
Communications and Release Managers coordinate release planning and external communications. They ensure stakeholders, customers, and support teams are informed of changes.

### Responsibilities
- Coordinate release planning and scheduling
- Draft and publish release notes
- Communicate releases to customers and support teams
- Coordinate with support on known issues and workarounds
- Manage release documentation and versioning

### Goals
- Enable smooth, well-communicated releases
- Reduce customer confusion and support tickets
- Maintain transparency and trust

### Typical Communication
- Release notes and customer announcements
- Pre-release stakeholder briefings
- Support team coordination and escalation

### Interaction with Existing Roles
- **With Project Managers**: Coordinate release scheduling and timeline
- **With Product Managers**: Get feature summaries and business context for release notes
- **With All Teams**: Gather release information and coordinate communication

---

## Security / Compliance Officer

### Role Summary
Security Officers ensure that security and compliance requirements are integrated into project execution. They conduct security reviews and manage risk mitigation.

### Responsibilities
- Define security requirements and review acceptance criteria
- Conduct security code reviews and architecture reviews
- Monitor compliance with organizational standards
- Manage security incidents and vulnerability responses
- Provide guidance on secure development practices

### Goals
- Prevent security breaches and compliance violations
- Build security into development practices
- Maintain organizational risk within acceptable levels

### Typical Communication
- Security reviews and threat assessments
- Compliance checklists and audit participation
- Incident response and post-mortems

### Interaction with Existing Roles
- **With Developers**: Review security designs; guide secure coding practices
- **With Project Managers**: Escalate security risks and compliance requirements
- **With Technical Leads**: Collaborate on security architecture and threat mitigation
